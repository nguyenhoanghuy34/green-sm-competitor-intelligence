## 🎯 Overview

**Green SM Competitor Intelligence** is an AI-powered agent designed to analyze **Green SM** and its competitors in the Vietnamese mobility market.

The project aims to collect and analyze publicly available information to identify:

- 📊 Competitor activities and market movements
- 📱 Content and marketing trends
- 💰 Pricing and promotional strategies
- 💬 Customer sentiment and feedback
- 💡 Competitive insights and opportunities

The initial competitor landscape includes **Grab, be, Gojek, Mai Linh, Vinasun, Ahamove, and Lalamove**.
